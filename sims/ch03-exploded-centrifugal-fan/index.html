    Exploded View of a Belt-Driven Centrifugal Fan¶

    Run the Exploded View of a Belt-Driven Centrifugal Fan MicroSim Fullscreen

    About This MicroSim¶

    This MicroSim shows a belt-driven single-width, single-inlet (SWSI) centrifugal fan in Arrangement 9, pulled apart along its shaft line the way an exploded view in FanPedia or a submittal drawing shows it. It names 14 parts, from the inlet collar and flanged inlet through the drive train to the pedestal.

    The drawing is a simplified schematic drawn in code, modeled on the FanPedia page 10 exploded view. It is not to scale and does not show a specific Twin City Fan model.

    The flanged inlet sits in its own "Inlet option" box because it is the alternative to the inlet collar. A fan ships with one or the other, not both.

    How to Use¶

    1. Explore mode (the starting mode). Select any part to open its infobox. Only one infobox is open at a time. The label of each part you have opened turns green, and the counter shows how many of the 14 you have visited.
    2. Assemble / Explode. Press Assemble to slide the parts into place. The inlet funnel and impeller disappear inside the housing, and the shaft, bearings, sheave, belts and motor drop onto the pedestal. Press Explode to pull them apart again. Labels are shown only in the exploded view.
    3. Quiz mode. Press Quiz. The labels are hidden and the drawing stays exploded. Ten prompts appear in a fixed order. Select the part that matches each one. You get two attempts per prompt:
      • Correct: you see "Correct:" with the reason, and that part's label appears.
      • First miss: you see which part you picked and the reason for the correct answer, then you try again.
      • Second miss: the correct part is highlighted in red and the prompt counts as missed.
    4. After the tenth prompt, a summary shows your score ("Correct on first attempt: n of 10") and lists the prompts you missed with their reasons. Mastery is 9 of 10 correct on the first attempt.
    5. Reset clears your visited parts and returns to explore mode.

    You can also move between parts with the Tab key and select one with Enter or Space.

    Lesson Plan¶

    Audience¶

    New application engineers, sales staff, manufacturer's representatives and facilities staff who will read exploded views and submittal drawings of centrifugal fans.

    Learning Objective¶

    Identify each of the 14 labeled parts in an exploded view of a belt-driven Arrangement 9 centrifugal fan when given the part's name or function (Bloom's Taxonomy: Remember, verb "identify").

    Duration¶

    10 to 15 minutes

    Prerequisites¶

    Read Chapter 3, Basic Fan Anatomy, through the drive-train sections. The MicroSim uses these terms: exploded view, fan housing, impeller, inlet funnel, inlet collar, inlet flange, outlet flange, shaft, bearing, sheave, V-belt, motor, slide base and pedestal.

    Activities¶

    1. Explore (5 min): Open all 14 infoboxes. For each part, say out loud whether it touches the air.
    2. Assemble and explode (2 min): Press Assemble and watch where the funnel, impeller and drive parts go. Note that the bearings, sheave and motor end up outside the housing.
    3. Quiz (5 min): Switch to quiz mode and answer the ten prompts without going back to explore mode.
    4. Review (3 min): Read the reasons for any missed prompts, return to explore mode for those parts, then retake the quiz.

    Common Misconceptions to Watch For¶

    • The inlet funnel and the inlet collar are the same part. The funnel shapes the air into the impeller; the collar is a duct connection.
    • The pedestal is part of the housing. It is a separate support for the bearings and shaft.
    • The fan sheave and the motor sheave are the same size. Their ratio sets fan speed.

    Assessment¶

    Mastery is 9 of 10 prompts correct on the first attempt in quiz mode. Opening infoboxes in explore mode is exploration and does not count as evidence.

    References¶

    2. Twin City Fan & Blower. FanPedia online. https://tcf.com/fanpedia/
