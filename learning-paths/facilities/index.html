    Plant and Facilities Staff¶

    This path is for you if you look after fans that are already installed and need to identify one, describe it correctly when you call for parts or a replacement, and recognize the hardware around it. It teaches you to name a fan's parts from an exploded view, to read its arrangement, rotation, discharge and motor nameplate, and to recognize its bearings and lubrication, shaft seals, drains, spark-resistant construction, vibration isolation and anti-backspin devices.

    This is the shortest path. It reads only the foundation and orientation chapters in full and picks single sections out of the hardware chapters. It does not teach maintenance or repair procedures, which are outside the scope of this book.

    Target Concepts¶

    These are the concepts that define the job outcome for this path. Everything else on the path is a prerequisite of one of them in the learning graph.

    Concept Taught in Why it is on this path
    Exploded View Ch. 7: Reading an Exploded View Remember: identify the major parts of a fan from an exploded view.
    Fan Arrangement Ch. 11: The Fan Arrangement (opening) Understand: describe how an arrangement determines where the motor, bearings and impeller sit.
    Rotation and Discharge Code Ch. 13: Rotation and Discharge Code (opening) Apply: write the complete rotation and discharge designation for a fan you are looking at.
    Motor Nameplate Ch. 14: The Motor Nameplate Topic 7: identify the installed motor before ordering a like-for-like replacement.
    Bearing Lubrication Ch. 16: Bearing Lubrication Topic 8: bearings and lubrication (oil mist, static oil, grease).
    Shaft Seal Ch. 16: Shaft Seals (opening) Evaluate: critique an accessory list, for example shaft seals for leak-tight service.
    Housing Drain Ch. 20: Housing Drain Evaluate: critique an accessory list for missing items, such as drains for wet service.
    Spark Resistant Construction Ch. 20: Spark Resistant Construction (opening) Remember and Understand: the three spark-resistant types and what each does and does not protect against.
    Vibration Isolation Ch. 22: Vibration Isolation (opening) Topic 12: isolation bases and isolators under installed fans.
    Anti-Backspin Device Ch. 23: Anti-Backspin Devices Topic 12: installation and support accessories (anti-backspin devices).

    When You Finish¶

    Walk up to a fan in your plant and write down its arrangement, rotation and discharge viewed from the drive side, and its motor nameplate line; practice the code first in the Rotation and Discharge Code Builder. If you order fans as well as maintain them, continue with the Inside Sales and Customer Service path, which adds model codes, catalogs and the complete fan description.
