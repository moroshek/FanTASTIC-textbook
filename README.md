## Overview

FanPedia is an interactive intelligent textbook on industrial and commercial fans and blowers. It is
built from Twin City Fan's *FanPedia: The Fan & Blower Encyclopedia* and current Twin City Fan
catalogs, and it is written for new application engineers, inside and outside sales staff,
manufacturer's representatives, distributors, specifying engineers, and plant or facilities staff
who need to identify, specify, or order fans.

The book moves from air, pressure and basic fan anatomy through fan families, impellers, drives and
arrangements, rotation and discharge, motors, bearings, inlets, outlets and flow control, special
construction, bases and vibration isolation, and fan laws and sound, and ends with reading catalogs
and writing a fan specification.

## What's in the Book

- **27 chapters**, each with a quiz and a curated reference list.
- **MicroSims**, interactive browser simulations embedded in the chapters (calculators,
  classifiers, explorers and selectors), plus interactive overlays on FanPedia diagrams that label
  each part and then quiz you on finding it.
- **Glossary** of 581 terms, one for every concept in the learning graph.
- **FAQ** with 98 questions and answers.
- **Learning graph** of 581 concepts and their prerequisites, with an interactive viewer.
- **Breeze the Hummingbird**, the book's mascot, who offers tips, warnings and encouragement
  along the way.

## Getting Started

- Browse chapters in order from the left sidebar, or use search to jump to a topic.
- Each MicroSim runs in your browser; open it full screen from the button under the simulation.
- Take the quiz at the end of each chapter to check your understanding.
